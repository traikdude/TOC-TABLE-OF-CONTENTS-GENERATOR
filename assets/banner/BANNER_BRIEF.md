# Return to the index

README hero and GitHub social preview for TOC-TABLE-OF-CONTENTS-GENERATOR. The audience is a Google Docs author navigating long, tabbed documents. The distinguishing gesture is the return trip from a heading to its own contents entry.

**Primary prompt.** Wide 2:1 editorial linocut illustration, overhead open ivory document folio on a deep midnight blue worktable. On the left page three indented contents rows represented by short ink strokes, with coral ochre and teal index marks. On the right stacked tabbed sheets with matching heading marks. Two elegant curving ribbons with opposite arrowheads connect one contents row to its corresponding heading and back. Hand printed texture, bold silhouettes, beautiful shadows, centered crop-safe composition. No lettering, words, numerals, logos or UI chrome.

**Alternate prompt.** Draw an editorial cut-paper spread with a stepped contents list on the left and tabbed pages on the right. Connect one selected row and its matching heading with two separate curved arrows facing opposite ways. Use warm ivory paper, midnight ink and coral and teal accents. Keep the endpoints inside the center crop. No text or logos.

**Exact text in art.** None.

**Alt text.** Two arrows connect a contents entry to its heading on a tabbed document and back.

**Placement.** Use `banner.png` above the README title and `social-preview.png` for the owner-uploaded share card. Both are 1280 × 640. The independent editable `banner.svg` uses an opaque background for light and dark pages. Refresh the art if heading navigation or tab scope changes.
